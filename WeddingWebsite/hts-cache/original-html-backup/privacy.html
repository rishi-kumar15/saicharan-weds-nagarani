1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to DreamsInvite (referred to as "we," "our," "us," or "the Platform"). DreamsInvite is premium digital invitation platform, creating interactive and highly immersive invitation websites for events.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the personal data provided to us by **Couples** (who create and host their invitations) and **Guests** (who view the invitations and submit RSVPs). By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to build, personalize, host, and manage your invitation website:

A. Information Provided by Couples

This includes details shared when ordering or customizing templates: names of the couple, family details, event descriptions, ceremony locations/addresses, dates and times, photos, background videos, music choices, and contact information (phone number, email address).

B. Information Submitted by Guests (RSVP Data)

When guests RSVP on a couple's wedding website, we collect their: Name, phone number, attendance status (Attending / Unsure), guest count, dietary preferences, and personal congratulatory wishes/messages.

3. How We Use the Information

We process the collected data for the following specific purposes:

  • To build, host, and publish your custom-designed interactive wedding invitation.
  • To store and securely present RSVP lists to the hosting couple for event planning and logistics.
  • To facilitate guest de-duplication so that couples have clean, accurate headcount data.
  • To communicate with couples regarding their orders, proof reviews, custom updates, and technical inquiries.
  • We strictly **NEVER sell, trade, rent, or lease** any personal couple or guest information to advertising agencies or third-party marketing services.

4. Guest RSVP Privacy & Access

Guest RSVPs are treated with strict confidentiality. All RSVP answers, phone numbers, and congratulations left by your guests on your wedding website are accessible **only by the hosting couple**.

Guests who register their information will not be contacted by DreamsInvite for advertising or marketing. Guest information is collected purely on behalf of, and for the sole utility of, the couple hosting the event.

5. Data Security Measures

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security controls to safeguard your data.

  • **Encryption in Transit**: All data passed between your browser and our servers is secured via 256-bit Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption.
  • **Secure Cloud Infrastructure**: RSVP data is stored in highly secure, firewalled cloud databases with restricted access credentials.
  • **No Financial Data Storage**: All order payments are handled via secure external gateway integration. We never process or store your credit/debit card numbers or net-banking details on our servers.

6. Data Retention, Hosting & Server Removal Policy

By default, active interactive invitation websites are kept online indefinitely (or for the duration specified in your package) so you can cherish and revisit your beautiful digital memories.
